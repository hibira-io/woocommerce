# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-02

- Live viewer count on product pages, with a threshold, and a store-wide count through `[hibira_viewers scope="store"]`.
- "Someone just bought" popups for paid orders, live and for recent orders, with no customer names or places.
- Live stock on open product pages, including a disabled add to cart button when a product sells out.
- Settings tab under WooCommerce → Settings with a test event, and `HIBIRA_APP_*` constants.
- Admin strings in English, 繁體中文, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, ภาษาไทย and Tiếng Việt.
